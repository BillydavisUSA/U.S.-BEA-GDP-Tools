# Code of Conduct

U.S. BEA GDP Tools welcomes contributors and users of every background and
experience level. This policy applies to the project's issues, pull requests,
reviews, and other project-managed community spaces. It also applies when
someone represents the project publicly.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate. Make room for different experiences.
- Critique ideas and code constructively; do not attack people.
- Accept corrections, acknowledge mistakes, and help newcomers participate.
- Respect others' privacy and give appropriate credit for their work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, personal attacks,
repeated disruption, and publishing someone's private information without
consent are unacceptable. Retaliation against someone reporting a concern is
also unacceptable.

## Reporting a concern

Contact the repository maintainer, [@BillydavisUSA](https://github.com/BillydavisUSA),
through the [project's GitHub issues](https://github.com/BillydavisUSA/U.S.-BEA-GDP-Tools/issues).
For a public report, describe the behavior and link to the relevant public
issue, comment, or pull request. Do not publish personal information, private
messages, credentials, or other sensitive evidence.

A dedicated private reporting address has not yet been published. If a concern
requires privacy, open an issue containing only a request for a private contact
channel and wait for the maintainer to provide one before sharing details. If
the concern involves the maintainer, use GitHub's abuse-reporting tools for
conduct that violates GitHub's policies.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews reports in context, hears relevant perspectives, and
shares sensitive information only as needed to address the incident. Depending
on severity and repetition, the maintainer may request a correction, remove
content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently
exclude a participant from project-managed spaces. Serious threats or sustained
harassment may result in immediate restrictions.

An affected participant may request reconsideration through the same reporting
route and provide new information. Moderation decisions must follow this policy
and must not penalize good-faith reporting.
